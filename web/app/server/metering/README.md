# The meter

Slatebox is free and open source, with no guest limits. Only two things cost money each time they are used, so only they are metered:

- **AI** (drafts, chat turns, welcome turns, presentation passes, generated images, image search), counted in dollars from the token counts `req.js` already writes to `AIAudit`.
- **Calls on Daily**, counted in participant-minutes and charged to the person who started the call.

Whoever starts a call or makes an AI request pays, in this order: their own key (or their team's), then the included amount, then a balance (theirs, then their team's). Guests and people who join a call never pay.

Own AI and call keys work on Slatebox Cloud as on a self-hosted Slatebox (owner, 2026-11-26; `imports/api/common/own-keys.mjs`). They are optional: a person or team adds an AI connection with its key in the AI tab (`ai.update`, `ai-settings-core.mjs`) or an AI or call key in Credits and team settings (`keys.save`), and binds any feature they may choose to it. A request their own key serves is routed `byok` (`policy.mjs route`, before the included amount): no Slatebox credits are spent, no Pro is needed, no hold is taken, and it has no per-request ceiling. A request on Slatebox's own models is metered exactly as before. The two cannot be mixed: an AI request is `byok` only when the person's or team's own choice serves that feature (`ownAIScope`, `credentialsFor`), and `req.js` refuses to send a `byok` request anywhere but that own connection (`ownKey && !chosen.own`); image search and Slatebox's image service inside it are counted as their own use (`chargeFixed`). Own keys are on where the vault has its master key and no administrator turned own AI off (`settings.ownKeys`, read with the meter's settings); then the refusals name them (`OWN_KEY_COPY`), else they leave them out (`COPY`). An administrator's off switch for a part of Slatebox, or for all AI, wins over an own choice. On Cloud an own connection is a public https address only, checked when saved and again by the lookup of every request (`endpoint-policy.mjs`); keys are sealed (`vault.mjs`), never returned, never logged or written to the audit trail, and saves are rate-limited.

The included amount ("Pro credits") is sized by one number, `free.creditCostMicrosPerMonth`: what full use may cost Slatebox in provider fees per person a month, $3.75 by default, so that full use never costs more than Pro brings in, even yearly Pro on an international card with currency conversion and tax on top (`scripts/stripe-catalog.test.mjs`). `sizeCredits` shares it out (AI 25%, voice 37.5%, video 37.5%): 30 drafts a day within about $0.94 of AI a month, 1,380 voice and 330 video participant-minutes (about 180 drafts, about 5½ hours of four-person voice and about 1 hour of four-person video, as separate allowances), with $0.50 a day per person. `proCreditsInWords` says the same in plain words for the profile, and `scripts/pro-credits.mjs` writes the same figures as `pro-credits.json`, the file the site and the docs copy and test against. Credits count per calendar month (UTC); the month Pro starts in, and the month it ends in once it will not renew, gives only the share of the month Pro covers (`monthShare`, `proCreditShare`), so buying on the 30th never brings two months of credits for one payment. Whether it needs **Slatebox Pro** (its prices in `imports/api/pro/prices.mjs`, the amounts people read on [the pricing page](https://slatebox.com/plans/); `server/pro-billing.mjs`) is `free.proRequiredFrom`: `'now'` on Slatebox Cloud (from 2026-11-22), `'never'` by default and on every self-hosted meter (self-host has no Pro), or a moment in ms. Without Pro where it is needed: included AI, image search and image generation and starting a call are refused with a clear message (`pro`, `pro-images`, `pro-calls`, `pro-past-due`) unless a balance pays; joining a call stays free; the free presentation plan stays at the guest caps (`guestPresentationsPerDay: 0` turns it off). Pro credits are never paused by the shared budget's stages; each person's day and month caps still hold. A self-hosted meter keeps `proRequiredFrom` at `'never'`, so the amount there is everyone's and Pro is not sold.

## The personalization allowance (owner, 2026-11-26)

On Slatebox Cloud each signed-in person (never a guest) gets a daily allowance, by UTC day, of the features that get to know them, and Slatebox pays for it: the interview in About you (`interview`, 20 turns a day; `interview.start` counts as a turn), For you ideas (`personal.suggest`, 5 runs a day) and building a For you idea (`personal.build`, 3 builds a day: `workshops.js draftForYou`, a board draft on the `workshop.draft` surface metered under its own key, so ordinary board drafts stay on `workshop` and outside the allowance; the build keeps the surface switch, the one-draft-a-minute lock and the quality gate). Another feature joins by being added to `PERSONAL_FEATURES` in `allowance.mjs`, as `profile.sources` and `profile.import` will. Within it a request is routed `allowance` (`policy.mjs route`, after an own key and before Pro credits): it runs on Slatebox's own binding, needs no Pro, charges nothing, and is recorded in `MeterEvents` with route `allowance` and on the global keys as `allowanceMicros` (by feature in `allowanceByFeature`) as well as `freeMicros`, so the shared budget's ceiling bounds it and **Admin → Budget and Pro** shows it. A request an own key serves is `byok` and never uses it; a person whose free use an administrator stopped, or a shared budget at its AI pause, has none. Past it the meter applies unchanged (Pro credits, a balance, an own key), and where that is a refusal for want of Pro the words say the allowance is used up (`usedUpWords`). Each use is taken with one conditional update on the person's day (`allowance_<feature>`), so two turns at the same moment cannot both take the last one; the losing request is decided again without the allowance. The caller's network address shares it out too (`address:<bucket>:<UTC day>`, `PEOPLE_PER_ADDRESS` people's worth, 5), so throwaway accounts made from one place get no more; a Cloudflare edge or the cluster's own address is not counted. From the shared budget's smaller-drafts stage (`smallerAiAt`) the allowance shrinks in the same proportion as free drafts. A request that fails before any usage was recorded, or that ran on the person's own connection and recorded nothing, gives its use back. Allowance spend counts in `freeByKind` too, and the admin's top spenders include it, so an account farming it can be found and stopped (`freeStopped`). The per-request ceilings and the rate limits stay as they are. Operators set the numbers with `metering.personalAllowance` (`{ "interview": 20, "personal.suggest": 5, "personal.build": 3 }`, checked at start; 0 turns one off); a stored `personalAllowance` in the meter's settings document wins. Self-host has no meter, so nothing changes there.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `prices.mjs` | Price table (source and read date per row), estimates, per-request ceilings. The operator adds or reprices models in `settings.metering.prices` (`[{ model, inPerMillion, outPerMillion }]`, checked at start). An unknown model is priced at the dearest row. |
| `policy.mjs` | The included amounts (`sizeCredits`, `resolveMetering`), the Pro switch, the route decision, budget stages, the daily ceilings, the summary people see. |
| `allowance.mjs` | The personalization allowance: its defaults (the one place they live), the features it pays for (`PERSONAL_FEATURES`), the used-up words, and its atomic hold and release. |
| `context.mjs` | `runAIAction` (AsyncLocalStorage). `req.js` refuses to send anything when the meter is on and no action is running, or after it ended. Work a request starts without waiting (`keepActionOpen`) keeps it open. Attempts that got no usage back are charged the feature estimate. |
| `meter-core.mjs` | The meter itself: route, hold, record, release. Tests run it with an in-memory store. |
| `holds.mjs` | Before a request is sent, its most possible spend is set aside with conditional updates (person's day and month, the global month up to 110%, or the wallet), so parallel requests cannot pass a cap. |
| `limits.mjs` | Per-person and per-address rate limits on every metered method. The per-person rule counts signed-in people only; the per-address rule never counts a Cloudflare edge address or a private one (the cluster's own: the ingress, a node, a pod). |
| `client-address.mjs` | The caller's address behind Cloudflare and the ingress (walks X-Forwarded-For left past Cloudflare's own ranges). `meteor.js` sets it on each connection. |
| `refusal-notice.mjs` | The meter's refusal goes into a slate's chat only for a signed-in caller who may change that saved slate. |
| `own-endpoint.mjs` | A person's own model endpoint: public https only, checked on save and on every connection. |
| `record.mjs` | One metered use: event (unique `ref`), counters, wallet debit. |
| `ledger.mjs` | Wallet arithmetic: balance never below zero, what can go back to a card, top-up limits, card fee, refund plan. |
| `calls.mjs` | Room per call, tokens, the 60-second tick (charge, extend, move to voice, end). Transcription (Slatebox's Deepgram key) only on unmetered operator calls: never on an own Daily key, and not on metered calls until it is priced. |
| `../voice/meter.mjs` | Talking with the agent (voice, `server/voice`): each minute of a session is one meter action, held before it starts and released at its end; every usage report from the provider is recorded at the voice model's audio and text prices (`prices.mjs` `VOICE_PRICES`, or the model row's own). Voice is never paid from a free amount: Pro credits, a balance or the person's own key (`policy.mjs` `VOICE_METER_FEATURES`, refusal `pro-voice`). A minute that cannot be held, or spends past its ceiling, ends the session (`closing { reason: 'cap' }`). |
| `vault.mjs` | AES-256-GCM for own keys, bound to owner and provider. Master key from `SLATEBOX_KEY_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (32 bytes, base64). |
| `migrations.mjs` | M1 (indexes and the settings document, at startup), M3 (field cleanup, owner-run) and M4 (Slatebox Pro indexes, at startup; no data change). |
| `meteor.js` | Meteor glue: settings, methods (`meter.*`, `keys.*`), the tick runner lease, alerts. |
| `../methods/wallet.js`, `../wallet-stripe.mjs` | Top-ups (Stripe Checkout, one-time), refunds, charges, webhook events. |
| `../methods/pro.js`, `../pro-billing.mjs` | Slatebox Pro: checkout (subscription, Stripe Tax on top, no trial), customer portal, the webhook's Pro events (state read fresh from Stripe, guarded by `fetchedAt`). Prices by lookup_key from `imports/api/pro/catalog.mjs`; Stripe objects made by `scripts/stripe-catalog.mjs` (Pro only). Slatebox Business (`imports/api/business/catalog.mjs`, per person inside Cloud) is `../methods/business.js` and `../business-billing.mjs`: a team owner or admin buys it on the team page at the two Stripe prices `settings.payments.business.monthly` / `.yearly` name (quantity = the team's members who have joined: invitations not yet accepted are not billed; monthly changes are prorated on the next invoice, members added to a yearly plan are invoiced at once for the rest of the year, `prorationFor`); the webhook writes `organizations.business` from the subscription; a staff-written contract is never overwritten. Without both price ids it is sold by talking to us (`BUSINESS_CONTACT_URL`). |

## Settings

`Meteor.settings.metering.enabled === true` turns the meter on, and only on Slatebox Cloud: the server must also be Cloud by its signed Cloud key (`public.edition: "cloud"` and `settings.enterprise.license` of kind `cloud` for its host, `server/cloud-lock.mjs`, the same proof invited agents need). Anywhere else there is no meter and no payments (Pro, Business, top-ups, balances, the Stripe webhook answers 404 and every payment method refuses with `cloud_only`), whatever the settings say, and the server logs one `[payments]` line at start saying which it is. It is read once at startup and fails closed: with `public.env` `prod` the server does not start unless it is `true`; with any env but `selfhost` and `dev` (prod, test, a missing one), `true` without an accepted Cloud key for the host stops the start rather than running unmetered; with any other env, including a missing or misspelled one, it must be `true` or `false`. Only `selfhost` and `dev` may leave it out, and then run with no meter: the operator's AI provider and Daily key are used with no limits. The numbers are resolved by `resolveMetering` in `policy.mjs`: the defaults (`DEFAULT_METERING`), then the deploy settings `metering.proRequiredFrom` (`"now"`, `"never"` or `"YYYY-MM-DD"`) and `metering.creditCostMicrosPerMonth` (checked at start: a wrong value stops it), then `SystemSettings` `_id: 'metering'` (created by M1), which admins change from **Admin → Budget and Pro** (the monthly budget, the hold, when Pro is needed, and what Pro credits may cost a month). Slatebox Cloud's settings carry `"metering": {"enabled": true, "proRequiredFrom": "now"}`; self-host leaves `proRequiredFrom` out.

Defaults: Pro credits sized to $3.75 a month (30 AI drafts a day with a $0.9375 monthly AI ceiling, 1,380 voice and 330 video participant-minutes a month), $0.50 a day per person for AI and call starts, a $1,000 monthly free budget with stages at 50/70/90/100/110% and a daily ceiling of P/20, top-ups at cost plus 5%.

Other settings: `googleImageSearch.{apiKey,engineKey}` (private; Google closes the API on 2027-01-01), `stripe.apiBase` and `daily.apiBase` (local stubs), `stripe.proPortalConfiguration` (optional `bpc_…`, else the account's default portal), `metering.alertEmail`, and in development only `ai.stubEndpoint`.

## Slatebox Pro (owner, 2026-11-22)

1. The lead runs `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=… node scripts/stripe-catalog.mjs --dry-run --live` with the live key (Slatebox Pro and its portal: product `slatebox_pro`, prices `pro_monthly_usd_500` and `pro_yearly_usd_5000`, tax-exclusive), shows the owner the plan, then runs it again without `--dry-run`. Without a key nothing runs, not even the dry run. Slatebox Business is not in the catalogue script: the owner makes its product and two prices in Stripe by hand and puts their ids in `settings.payments.business`; nothing else is created.
2. In the Stripe dashboard, check every line of the run's `dashboardChecks` (the script cannot set them, and the terms rely on them): failed payments **cancel** the subscription after the last retry (not "mark as unpaid"), upcoming-renewal emails are on for yearly Pro, failed-payment emails link to the portal, Stripe Tax is on, the webhook receives the subscription, invoice, checkout, refund and dispute events. If a subscription is ever left `unpaid` anyway, the app treats it as still held (no second checkout; a second subscription is cancelled as a duplicate).
3. Switch now (owner, 2026-11-22: no users yet, no notice period, no trial): set `"proRequiredFrom": "now"` in Cloud's deploy settings (`metering`) with the app release. Pro goes on sale at once (`proOnSale`) and is paid from the first day. Then switch on the site's and the docs' `pro-checkout`, so "Get Slatebox Pro" never leads to a Credits tab with no Pro on it.

Pro credits are spent on the global keys as `proMicros` (and held as `heldProMicros`), never as the `freeMicros` and `heldMicros` the shared budget's stages, daily rule and alerts read: paying people's use never pauses free use. **Admin → Budget and Pro** shows Pro credits spent beside the shared budget. Only paying people skip the stages: live Pro where Pro is needed.

Account removal: the balance is refunded first, then Pro is ended (no refund of the current period), then the account is removed. A failed refund leaves Pro on and removes nothing.

A dispute hold belongs to the subscription it was raised on: a later subscription (bought again after a lost dispute) starts without it.

## Behind Cloudflare

Per-address limits need the caller's address. `HTTP_FORWARDED_COUNT=1` makes the ingress's own X-Forwarded-For entry the trusted one, and `client-address.mjs` walks left past Cloudflare's ranges to the visitor. That only works if the ingress keeps the entry Cloudflare sends: configure the ingress to trust X-Forwarded-For from Cloudflare's ranges (Traefik `forwardedHeaders.trustedIPs`). Until then the only address seen is Cloudflare's or the ingress's own private one, and the per-address rules skip both (per-person limits, the confirmed-address rule and the caps still apply), so guests behind the ingress are never put in one count.
